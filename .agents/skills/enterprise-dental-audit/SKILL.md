# Enterprise Dental Clinic Quality & Ergonomic Standard

## 1. Clinical Charting & Diagnostics
- **FDI & Palmer Dual Notation**: Automatic conversion and clear quadrant markers (┘ | └, ┐ | ┌).
- **MODBL Anatomical Surfaces**: Mesial, Occlusal, Distal, Buccal, Lingual surface tagging.
- **6-Point Periodontal Probing**: Recording Pocket Depth (PPD), Bleeding on Probing (BOP), Furcation, and Mobility.
- **Pediatric & Deciduous Dentition**: Clear distinction of primary teeth (51 to 85, A through E).
- **Implant Passport (ITI Standard)**: Fixture lot numbers, dimensions (diameter/length), platform type, insertion torque.

## 2. Reception & Operatory Workflow
- **Multi-Chair Scheduling**: Dedicated unit tracking (Blue, Yellow, Purple chairs) with doctor collision prevention.
- **Waiting Room Lounge & Audio Callout**: Web Speech API Persian vocalization of ticket numbers and unit assignments.
- **Smart Waiting List Backfill**: Automatic candidate suggestion upon appointment cancellation.
- **Patient Presence Lifecycle**: Timestamp recording of `check_in_time`, `chair_entry_time`, and `chair_exit_time`.

## 3. Financial & Legal Governance
- **Sayad Cheque Engine**: 16-digit identifier verification, due dates, bank routing, clearing statuses.
- **Doctor Commission Calculations**: Guaranteed pre-deduction of dental lab costs and implant hardware exclusions before revenue split.
- **Thermal POS Slip**: 80mm receipt generation with terminal IDs, RRN, and breakdown.
- **Digital Informed Consent**: Touch/stylus signature canvas with timestamp and procedure risk disclosure.

## 4. Visual & Tactile Ergonomics
- **Sterile Glove Ergonomics**: Minimum 48px interactive touch targets on operatories.
- **Living Gemini Mesh Background**: Dynamic, subtle fluid motion tinted with module identity.
- **Cupertino Glass & Haptics**: Tactile feedback and acoustic chimes for clinical confirmations and safety warnings.
